# The Economic Ripple Effect of Food Waste 🍽️

**UKM Data Challenge 5.0 | Team: Kaw Kaw Penguins**

## Project Overview
This project explores the relationship between household food expenditure, inflation, and food waste in Malaysia. Through data analysis, forecasting, and interactive visualizations, it aims to highlight the economic impact of food waste and promote sustainable consumption.

## Objectives
- Analyze food waste trends across Malaysian states.
- Explore the relationship between household income, food expenditure, CPI, and inflation.
- Forecast future food waste and expenditure trends.
- Develop an interactive dashboard to visualize findings and potential cost savings.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Programming & Analysis:** R, Microsoft Excel
- **Data Visualization:** Power BI
- **Forecasting:** Time-Series Analysis
- **Data Source:** Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM)

## Methodology
1. Data collection and preprocessing
2. Exploratory data analysis
3. Analysis of food waste, expenditure, and inflation trends
4. Time-series forecasting
5. Interactive Power BI dashboard development

## Key Findings
- Food waste accounts for approximately **44.4%** of Malaysia's household waste composition.
- Selangor, Johor, and Sabah were identified as major contributors to food waste.
- Rising food prices do not necessarily lead to lower food waste.
- Developed an interactive food waste calculator to estimate potential household savings.
- Projected approximately **5.1 billion kg of food waste by 2034** under the forecasting scenario.

## Project Outputs
- Six-page interactive Power BI dashboard
- Food waste cost and savings calculator
- Research poster and presentation slides
